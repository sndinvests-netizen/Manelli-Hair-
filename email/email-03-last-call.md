## Design Notes
- **Background:** #3a4a24 (deep green) for header block; #efe8e2 (cream) for body
- **CTA button:** #8f8554 (muted gold)
- **Code display:** Large, bold, center-aligned in cream text on green background
- **Product grid:** 3 cards, minimal copy — name + one-line character descriptor + CTA
- **Send condition:** Only to openers from Email 2 who have NOT completed a purchase. Suppress all purchasers and non-openers.
- **Tone check:** Read the final email aloud. If it sounds like begging, rewrite it. It should sound like a friend giving you one final, calm heads-up — and then walking away.
